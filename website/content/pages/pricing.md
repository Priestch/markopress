## FAQ

### General Questions

**Is MarkoPress really free?**

Yes! The Free plan is free forever for personal projects. You can build unlimited pages and posts with all core features.

**Can I switch plans later?**

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll only be charged the prorated difference.

**What payment methods do you accept?**

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal. For Enterprise plans, we also support wire transfers and invoices.

### Features

**Do I need coding skills?**

Not necessarily! If you can write markdown, you can use MarkoPress. However, custom themes and plugins do require HTML/CSS/JavaScript knowledge.

**Can I use my own domain?**

Custom domains are available on Pro and Enterprise plans. We provide step-by-step guides for connecting your domain.

**Is there a limit on traffic or bandwidth?**

No! We don't limit traffic or bandwidth on any plan. Your site can handle as much traffic as it gets.

**Can I export my content?**

Yes! Your content is yours. You can export all your pages and posts as markdown files anytime.

### Support

**What kind of support do you offer?**

- **Free:** Community support (Discord, GitHub Issues)
- **Pro:** Email support with 24-hour response time
- **Enterprise:** 24/7 phone support with dedicated success manager

**Do you offer refunds?**

Yes! We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for Pro plans. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund.

**Do you provide training?**

Training is included with Enterprise plans. For Pro and Free plans, check out our comprehensive documentation and community resources.

### Technical

**What hosting platforms do you support?**

MarkoPress works with any static hosting service:
- Vercel
- Netlify
- GitHub Pages
- GitLab Pages
- AWS S3 + CloudFront
- And many more!

**Can I self-host?**

Yes! Self-hosting is available on Enterprise plans with on-premise deployment options.

**Do I need a server?**

No! MarkoPress builds static files that can be hosted anywhere. No server required.

### Security

**Is my data secure?**

Absolutely. All sites are built as static files, making them inherently secure. Enterprise plans include additional security features like SSO and advanced access controls.

**Do you backup my content?**

Your content is stored in your Git repository, so you have full control over backups. We recommend using your Git provider's backup features.

**What about GDPR?**

MarkoPress is GDPR compliant. We don't track users or collect personal data. You have full control over any analytics you integrate.

## Migration

### Coming from Other Platforms?

**Migrating from VitePress?**

Your VitePress content is fully compatible! Use our [migration guide](./docs/migration) to switch in minutes.

```bash
npx @markopress/migrate@latest from-vitepress
```

**Migrating from Docusaurus?**

We have a one-click migration tool that preserves your content and structure.

```bash
npx @markopress/migrate@latest from-docusaurus
```

**Custom migration?**

Our team can help with custom migrations. Contact us at [migration@markopress.dev](mailto:migration@markopress.dev)
